==================== v7.5 (new bg video, upscaled; volume 0.3) ====================
Background video: bg4.mp4 / bg4-720.mp4 / poster4.jpg (replaces bg3*; bg3 entry commented out in config.js)
  Source: video supplied by the site owner (1920x1080, 25 fps, AAC), full length 3:51.6, audio kept.
  Upscale: ffmpeg hqdn3d (light denoise) -> lanczos 2560x1440 -> unsharp, libx264 High, 2-pass 3.0 Mbps,
           AAC 128k, +faststart. bg4.mp4 = 2560x1440 25 fps, 90.7 MB.
  Real-ESRGAN was NOT used: on this CPU-only box realesr-general-x4v3 measured 1.26 s/frame (360p->1440p),
           3.02 s/frame (540p->2160p), 11.56 s/frame from 1080p -> ~18.6 h for the 5790 frames. 4K would not fit
           the 95 MB per-file limit at acceptable quality either, so the target is 2K (1440p).
  bg4-720.mp4 (mobile): 1280x720 25 fps, x264 High crf 24 / maxrate 1.5 Mbps, AAC 128k, 40.7 MB.
  poster4.jpg: frame at 00:01, 1920 wide.
  Focus map (src/focus.js): regenerated for bg4 with YuNet face detection (small/bottom-band/crowd faces ignored).
Audio: default volume 0.3 (was 0.6); the unmute fade targets 0.3; volume is stored under the new key "sv.vol"
       (the old "sv.volume" is removed once, so returning visitors also get 0.3).
Removed: the subtitle line under the nick (index section) — HTML, CSS and tests; nothing replaces it.
Fix — liquid glass "pulling in late" after landing on work/contact:
  Cause: the engine faded each section with opacity (<1, e.g. 0.85-0.999 while S.p eased in) plus a blur filter during
  the transition. An ancestor with opacity < 1 or a filter becomes a backdrop root, so the backdrop-filter of .ct-col /
  .presence / the stack pill only saw the transparent section and showed the sharp video; the frost appeared only once
  the section opacity read exactly 1, ~1-1.5 s after the transition had landed.
  Fix: sections that contain glass are never faded/filtered themselves - the fade goes onto their glass elements and
  glass-free sub-trees, the transition blur only onto the glass-free parts, the section keeps only the transform; and the
  landed section reaches opacity 1 by the end of the transition (no longer waiting for the eased S.p).
  Second cause (work panels): the camera / panel layout followed the damped S.p, which kept settling ~1.5 s after the
  0.85 s transition (p 0.89 at the end of it), so the WebGL glass panels kept drifting and brightening into place
  (panel glass luma 183 -> 128 over 1.6 s). Now S.p keeps the same early motion but lands exactly with the transition,
  the panels' glass strength is tied to the transition, and the fly-in starts with the transition.
  Verified with stepped 1/30 s frame captures (test/probe_glass.py) in Chromium and WebKit: after landing the glass metrics
  (backdrop luma / std inside .ct-col and the first work panel) stay constant within ~1-2 %, panel bbox settles within 4 frames.
